
Privacy Policy for SmartChess

Last Updated: November 8, 2026
The developer of SmartChess ("the Game") is committed to protecting the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the Game.
By downloading or using the Game, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

• Offline Mode: SmartChess operates primarily offline. The Game does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data (such as your name, email address, or location) to external servers.
• Game Progress (20 Levels): All your game progress and unlocked levels after winning are stored locally on your device only.
• Local Network Play (LAN): When playing against another device via a local network, move data is transmitted directly between the two devices and does not pass through any external servers.
• Advertisements: The Game is completely ad-free and does not contain any third-party advertising tracking tools.
• Future Online Mode: The online multiplayer feature is currently disabled ("Available in the next update"). When this feature is launched in the future, this policy will be updated to explain the limited data required to operate online play (such as a username of your choice and matchmaking rating).

2. Data Security

Since all game data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on your personal device's safety. We do not have any access to your device or the data stored on it.

3. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when introducing the "Online Mode" in future updates. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
